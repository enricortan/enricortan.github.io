FlightDesk does the checking for a family trip: it searches the routes, scores what it finds and tells me when something fits the budget.

**It runs on my laptop.** A Node.js app with no dependencies and no install step. Double-click and it opens in its own browser window.

**It ranks, it doesn't just list.** Every itinerary gets a score from price, schedule and duration (50/30/20), converted to Philippine pesos. Preferred departure and arrival windows can either boost matching flights or hide the rest.

**It knows what's live and what's a guess.** Live fares come from Google Flights, read page by page, including the full-service airlines that only appear after the page's scripts run. Rows from AI web research or reference tables are tagged as estimates. They never trigger a price-drop alert, and they raise at most one "fits your budget" alert per airline and route per day.

**It taps you on the shoulder.** Alerts for a fare under budget, a drop of a set percentage, or a new promo that fits the trip arrive as Windows notifications. Anything raised during quiet hours waits until morning.

**The chat can change the settings, and says so.** "Only show departures between 8 am and 2 pm" changes the filter. The chat uses the Anthropic API, or the local Claude Code CLI when there's no key, and always says what it changed.
