## Context

CIMB Pay is the QR payments part of the CIMB Bank PH app. Its key visual calls it "The new CIMB Pay", set against a fast-food counter, a clothing store and a supermarket aisle.

The scanner's header lists the networks it reads: QR Ph, InstaPay, GCash and Maya. Two lines under the viewfinder say what each code is for:

- Look for **QR Ph** codes for merchant and bill payments.
- Look for **InstaPay** codes for peer-to-peer fund transfer.

## Key decisions, as the screens show them

{/* TODO(Enrico): for each decision, add the why and the trade-off in your words. Nothing is inferred beyond what's on screen. */}

### 1. One scanner, explained on the scanner

Merchant payments and person-to-person transfers share one camera. The screen names the networks it reads and says, in one line each, which code is for what.

### 2. Limits and fees before you commit

The fee ("No Fee") sits under the amount, and the day's InstaPay limit ("0 out of 5") shows on the same screen. Both are visible before Continue.

### 3. Goal money is flagged

When you choose where to pay from, an account tied to an OMG savings goal is labelled "Linked to a goal" in red.

### 4. Review, then MPIN, then a receipt

Every detail is shown once more on its own Review screen before the MPIN, and the success screen repeats the whole receipt with a reference number.

### 5. A receipt you'd send

"Share as a Card" turns the receipt into a card: a themed header (a birthday, the holidays), an editable title and message, and the transfer details underneath, marked "Shared from CIMB Pay". My QR works the same way: swipe to change its background, then share it.
