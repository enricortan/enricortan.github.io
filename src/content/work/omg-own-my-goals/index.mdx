## Context

OMG! Own My Goals is a savings feature inside the CIMB Bank PH app. In the app's home screen it has its own entry, "Own My Goals! Start your savings goals today!", between the quick actions and the account tiles.

Its landing makes four promises, in this order:

1. Achieve your goals faster with high-interest savings products.
2. Stay on track using Auto Save and Reminder features.
3. Personalize your goals with your preferred templates.
4. Celebrate your goal milestones.

Below them it points to three of the bank's savings products: GSave, UpSave and GROW.

## The flow

The screens walk through one goal from start to finish:

- **Start.** The landing, then a dashboard of active goals. Each goal is a card with its own icon, a progress ring, the days left, the amount saved out of the target, and a "You're on track" status.
- **Name it.** "Choose a Savings Theme": a carousel of categories, each with its own 3D object and background colour, from a new gadget and a wedding to tuition. A custom colour and a custom emoji are one tap away.
- **Plan it.** "Set your savings frequency": weekly, every other week or monthly. The screen shows how much to save per period to reach the target, with all three options compared underneath.
- **Automate it.** "Auto save and reminder": Auto save comes first and moves money on schedule from an account you pick. Manual, with reminders, is the alternative.
- **Watch it.** The goal's page: days left, Cash-in and Withdraw, progress against the target, and four milestones along the way.
- **Finish it.** "Goal Achieved!", with a trophy and fireworks, then a choice: view the goal's details or open a new goal.

## Key decisions, as the screens show them

### 1. A goal has a face

Every goal gets a theme before it gets a number: an object and a colour for travel, tuition or a car, or your own colour and emoji. The goal's object follows it onto the dashboard card and its detail page.

### 2. The app does the arithmetic

The frequency screen leads with the answer, the amount to save per period, and lists what the other two frequencies would cost underneath.

### 3. Automatic first, manual second

Auto save is the first and larger option, with the source account and the schedule on the same card. Manual saving, with reminders, sits under it.

### 4. Progress is something to celebrate

Goals carry milestones and an on-track status, and finishing one gets a full-screen celebration.

### 5. Goals sit on real products

OMG points to the bank's savings products, GSave, UpSave and GROW, from its landing and its promo screen. In the CIMB Pay designs, an account used by a goal is flagged "Linked to a goal" when you pick where to pay from.
